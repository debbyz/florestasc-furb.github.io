FlorestaSC (Forest and Floristic Inventory of Santa Catarina) collects up-to-date, detailed and reliable information on the quantity and quality of the forests of Santa Catarina. The programme has three lines of action:

Survey and monitoring of tree species diversity and biomass stocks in the forests of Santa Catarina Learn more →

Mapping of forest cover and other land uses with GIS and remote sensing techniques Learn more →

Science communication and environmental education initiatives that value forests, aimed especially at schools Learn more →

FlorestaSC was created to build a solid database to:

  • Support public policies on the use and conservation of the state's forests;
  • Provide information for ecological-economic zoning of forestry in the state;
  • Generate new information to update the list of threatened plant species;
  • Help identify and define priority areas for conservation and for the restoration of degraded ecosystems.

The studies carried out by FlorestaSC – 1) Inventory of forest remnants; 2) Forest cover monitoring; 3) Allometry; 4) Herbarium; 5) Genetic diversity; and 6) Socio-environmental survey – produce the following products:

1) Technical and scientific publications on species composition, structure (diameter, horizontal and vertical) and forest dynamics, based on data from the systematic, continuous forest inventory (see publications and products);

2) Maps of the native forests of Santa Catarina and other land uses based on remote sensing (see here);

3) Statistical models to predict wood volume and biomass of the natural forests of Santa Catarina (see here);

4) Botanical collections made available in the herbarium and online platforms (see here);

5) Genetic diversity and structure of threatened species populations using allozyme markers (see here);

6) Traditional uses of forest resources and the views of the rural population, based on interviews (see here);

Since 2007, the IFFSC has used a method compatible with that of the Brazilian National Forest Inventory (IFN), adapted to the state's specific needs. It is based on systematic sampling on multiple occasions, repeated every five years, to produce information and monitor the conservation status of forest resources and land-use change over time. The IFFSC is a permanent programme of the State Government.

The first field measurements (Cycle 1) took place from 2007 to 2011, with 597 plots surveyed across the state. Plot re-measurement (Cycle 2) began in 2014 and runs over 5 years, with 20% of the plots re-measured each year.

Run by the Regional University of Blumenau (FURB) in partnership with other universities in the state – Santa Catarina State University (UDESC) and the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC) – and with the Santa Catarina Agricultural Research and Rural Extension Company (Epagri).

Funded by the Santa Catarina Research and Innovation Foundation (FAPESC), the State Government and the Brazilian Forest Service (SFB).

The information produced by the IFFSC is public and is available through publications, databases and systems.
